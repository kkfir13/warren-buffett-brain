POLICY · UPDATED 5 NOVEMBER 2026

Privacy and data handling.

Warren Buffett Brain is an independent open-source research project. Historical research on the website and in the bundled skill requires no Buffett Brain account, financial credentials or brokerage connection.

Bundled research and AI conversations

The research library is included in the skill package. Using it does not itself send your portfolio or query to a Buffett Brain-operated database. Your AI provider may process prompts, retrieved source content and conversations according to its own terms and privacy settings.

Optional ValuEdge MCP-backed plugin

A separate, MCP-enabled Warren Buffett Brain package can connect to the ValuEdge remote MCP server at https://valuedge.app/mcp. When your assistant calls its public tools, ValuEdge receives the relevant research request, such as ticker symbols, company names, valuation queries or screening parameters, and returns its public stock research data. The public tools do not require a ValuEdge sign-in.

ValuEdge's private portfolio, watchlist and Smart Alerts tools require your own account authorization and eligible subscription. When you explicitly request those tools, ValuEdge can return information from your authorized account to the AI assistant. The MCP server is read-only; it does not place trades or modify holdings. Installing the historical research skill alone does not authorize private ValuEdge access.

ValuEdge is a separate commercial research service operated by this project's maintainer. For details about data categories, security, technical records, retention, third parties and your choices, consult the ValuEdge Privacy Policy and ValuEdge Terms of Service. Your chosen AI provider also processes MCP tool requests and responses under its own policies. You can disconnect the MCP integration through supported client settings.

Local MCP

The optional desktop-only open-source MCP adapter reads public research notes on your own device. It does not require a hosted ValuEdge connection. An independently hosted copy of the public Worker may have separate network logs determined by its operator.

Website and third parties

The public research website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process normal website access records. Following source links takes you to external publishers, subject to their policies.

Support

Report issues at GitHub Issues. Do not post private account or brokerage information in a public issue.
